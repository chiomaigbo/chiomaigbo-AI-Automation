## Featured Projects

### 1. AI Business Expense & Financial Document Assistant

**Project Overview**

Designed an AI-powered expense management solution to streamline expense submissions, document handling, expense categorization, approval routing, and financial reporting.

**Key Features**
- Digital expense submission and tracking
- AI-assisted expense categorization
- Receipt validation and clarification routing
- Automated approval routing based on expense thresholds
- Manager approval and rejection workflows
- Financial dashboard showing expense totals and approval statuses
- Automated email notifications and reporting

**Technologies:** Make, Gemini AI, Airtable, Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail, Lovable

**Business Value:** Reduces manual expense processing, improves visibility into financial transactions, and supports consistent approval decisions.

[View Project](./AI-Business-Expense-Assistant)

### 2. AI Customer Support Assistant

**Project Overview**

Developed an AI Customer Support Assistant interface designed to help customers submit inquiries about orders, billing, returns, and account-related issues.

The project also includes a separate conceptual Customer Complaint Management & Resolution Workflow, designed using Whimsical for a fictional banking organization.

**Key Features**
- Customer inquiry submission interface
- AI-assisted response drafting
- Conversation history display
- Proposed escalation to human support when required
- Complaint registration and severity assessment in the conceptual workflow
- Complaint investigation, resolution, and feedback process design

**Technologies:** Lovable, Whimsical, AI-assisted customer support design

**Business Value:** Demonstrates how AI-assisted customer support and structured complaint management can improve response consistency, reduce repetitive work, and support better customer experiences.

[View Project](./AI-Customer-Support-Assistant)

### 3. Business Solutions Inquiry Automation

**Project Overview**

Designed a business inquiry automation workflow using Make to capture incoming inquiries, store customer information, and route inquiries for appropriate follow-up.

**Key Features**
- Online inquiry collection through Typeform
- Automated record creation in Airtable
- Conditional routing based on inquiry classification
- Email notifications through Gmail
- Airtable record updates following workflow actions

**Technologies:** Make, Typeform, Airtable, Gmail

**Business Value:** Reduces manual inquiry handling, supports timely follow-up, and improves consistency in managing business leads and requests.
