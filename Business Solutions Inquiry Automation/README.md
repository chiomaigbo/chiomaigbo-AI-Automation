# Business Solutions Inquiry & Lead Management Automation

## Project Overview
Designed a business inquiry and lead management workflow using Make, Typeform, Airtable, and Gmail to automate inquiry collection, record creation, routing, and follow-up communication.

## Project Objectives
- Reduce manual processing of business inquiries.
- Improve lead tracking and response coordination.
- Support timely follow-up through automated notifications.

## Key Features
- Capture inquiries through Typeform.
- Automatically create lead records in Airtable.
- Route inquiries using conditional logic.
- Send email notifications through Gmail.
- Update Airtable records following workflow actions.

## Tools Used
Make | Typeform | Airtable | Gmail | Conditional Routing

## Business Value
Reduces repetitive data entry, improves inquiry organization, and supports consistent follow-up with prospective customers.

## Skills Demonstrated
Workflow Automation | Business Process Design | Conditional Routing | Data Integration | Lead Management | Customer Communication
